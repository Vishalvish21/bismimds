Beginners Guide

ആദ്യ ചുവടുകൾ

Steps to Get Your Driving License

01
Apply for Learner's License (LL)
Visit RTO or apply online at parivahan.gov.in. Carry Age Proof, Address Proof & Passport Photo.
02
Pass the LL Theory Test
Computer-based test on traffic signs, road rules, and motor vehicle act. Pass to get your Learner's License.
03
Complete 30 Days of Practice
Practice driving under supervision with your Learner's License. Bismi MDS provides expert-guided training.
04
Apply for Driving License (DL)
Apply after 30 days of LL. Appear for the practical driving test at the RTO ground.
05
Get Your Permanent DL
Upon passing, receive your permanent Driving License. Valid for 20 years (below 40) or up to age 60.

Documents Required (LL Application)

Age Proof
Aadhaar, Birth Certificate, or SSLC Certificate
Address Proof
Aadhaar Card, Ration Card, or Electricity Bill
Passport Size Photo
Recent, white background, 3.5×4.5 cm
Medical Certificate (Form 1A)
For HMV/Transport — Eye & Physical health check
Application Fee
₹200 for LL application (online or at RTO counter)

ROAD SAFETY

Rules of Driving

ഡ്രൈവിംഗ് നിയമങ്ങൾ

01
Always Wear Seatbelt
Driver and all passengers must wear seatbelts. Fine of ₹1,000 for violation under MV Act Section 194B.
02
Never Use Mobile While Driving
Use of handheld phone while driving attracts fine of ₹5,000. Use Bluetooth/speaker system only.
03
Obey Speed Limits
Urban roads: 50 km/h. Highways: 100 km/h (LMV). Overspeeding fines up to ₹2,000–₹5,000.
04
Do Not Drink & Drive
BAC above 30mg/100ml is illegal. Penalty: ₹10,000 and/or 6 months imprisonment. Zero tolerance.
05
Drive on Left Side
India follows left-hand traffic rules. Always keep left, overtake from the right side only.
06
Use Indicators for Turns
Signal at least 30 meters before turning or changing lanes. Failure to signal is a traffic violation.
07
Do Not Honk in Silent Zones
Avoid honking near hospitals, schools, and courts. Fine applicable under Motor Vehicles Act.
08
Maintain Safe Distance
Keep a safe gap from the vehicle ahead — minimum 3 seconds following distance on highways.
09
Stop at Red Signals
Never jump traffic signals. Fine ₹1,000–₹5,000. Three violations can lead to license cancellation.
10
Give Way to Emergency Vehicles
Ambulance, fire brigade, and police vehicles have right of way. Move to the left immediately.
01
Helmet is Mandatory
Both driver and pillion rider must wear ISI-certified helmets. Fine ₹1,000 per person without helmet.
02
Max One Pillion Rider
Only one pillion rider allowed on a motorcycle. Triple riding is illegal and attracts heavy penalty.
03
Speed Limit: 60 km/h Urban
Motorcycle speed limit is 60 km/h in urban areas and 80 km/h on highways unless otherwise marked.
04
No Stunts on Public Roads
Wheelies, stoppies, and stunts are illegal on public roads. Liable for imprisonment up to 3 months.
05
Use Both Brakes Properly
Apply front and rear brakes together. Sudden hard braking causes accidents. Practice progressive braking.
06
Wear Reflective Gear at Night
Use reflective jackets or vests when riding at night for better visibility to other road users.
07
Minimum Age 18 for MCWG
Valid MCWG license mandatory. Learner's license required during training period (carry it always).
08
Headlight ON Always
Mandatory to keep headlights on even during daytime as per Kerala Motor Vehicles rules.
01
All Passengers Must Wear Seatbelt
Mandatory for all occupants including rear passengers. Fine ₹1,000 per violation from 2019 amendment.
02
Max Occupancy Must Be Followed
Do not overload the vehicle beyond seating capacity. Overloading is a punishable offense.
03
Park Only in Designated Areas
Parking on yellow/double lines or no-parking zones attracts fines. Use designated parking spaces only.
04
Reverse Carefully with Warning
Sound horn before reversing. Reverse camera/sensor use recommended. Maintain clear visibility.
05
Children in Rear Seat
Children below 14 must be in rear seat only. Child safety locks to be used when children are passengers.
06
Use Headlights in Fog/Night
Switch to low beam in fog; use fog lights. High beam use within 200m of oncoming vehicle is prohibited.
07
No Tinted Glass Beyond Limits
VLT (Visible Light Transmission) minimum 70% for windshield. Tinting beyond limits attracts ₹1,000 fine.
08
Maintain Fitness Certificate
Vehicle fitness certificate mandatory for vehicles over 15 years. Must be renewed annually.
01
Valid HMV License Mandatory
Separate HMV endorsement on license required. Driving without valid HMV license attracts ₹10,000 fine.
02
Weight Limits Strictly Followed
Overloading beyond GVW is heavily penalized. ₹2,000 for first 1,000 kg excess, ₹1,000/1,000 kg thereafter.
03
No Driving Beyond 8 Hours
HMV drivers must not drive more than 8 hours without a break. Fatigue is the leading cause of HMV accidents.
04
Mandatory Reflective Markings
Yellow reflective tape on rear and sides of trucks mandatory for night visibility under CMVR rules.
05
Route Permits Required
Transport vehicles must carry valid route permits. Operating without permit: fine ₹5,000 and vehicle seizure.
06
Speed Limit: 60 km/h on Highways
Heavy vehicles have lower speed limits. Maximum 60 km/h on national highways, 40 km/h urban areas.
07
Regular Vehicle Fitness Check
Annual fitness renewal mandatory for HMV. Brakes, lights, tyres must be inspected regularly.
08
Hazardous Goods Special License
Drivers carrying flammable/toxic goods need special HazMat endorsement license and training.
Documents Every Driver Must Carry While Driving
Driving License (Original)
Valid DL with correct endorsement. Digital DL on DigiLocker accepted.
Registration Certificate (RC)
Vehicle RC Book or Smart Card RC. Available on DigiLocker or mParivahan.
Insurance Certificate
Valid third-party or comprehensive insurance. Driving without insurance: ₹2,000 fine + license suspension.
Pollution Under Control (PUC)
Valid PUC certificate mandatory. Fine ₹1,000 for expired PUC. Renewal at fuel stations.
Fitness Certificate (FC)
For HMV and transport vehicles — annual fitness certificate from designated inspection centers.
Route Permit (For Transport)
Commercial vehicles need valid permit matching the route of operation.
TIP: Download the mParivahan or DigiLocker app — all documents accepted digitally by Kerala Traffic Police!

Driving License Renewal

When to Renew
📅
Before Expiry (30 days prior)
Apply for renewal 30 days before your DL expires. No fine if renewed within the window.
⚠️
Grace Period (1 Year)
DL can be renewed up to 1 year after expiry with a late fee. After 1 year, fresh driving test required.
🔁
Validity
DL valid for 20 years or up to age 40 (whichever earlier). After 40, valid till age 60. Then every 5 years.
Documents Required
Existing DL (Original)
Your current/expired driving license
Passport Photo (2 copies)
Recent white background photo
Medical Certificate (Form 1)
From registered MBBS doctor (for HMV and age 40+)
Renewal Fee
₹200 (LMV) | ₹300 (HMV) online at parivahan.gov.in

Vehicle Insurance Renewal

Types of Insurance
🔴
Third-Party Insurance (Mandatory)
Covers damages to third party only. Legally required. Penalty ₹2,000 for no insurance. Renewed annually.
🟢
Comprehensive Insurance
Covers own damage + third party. Better protection. Recommended for all vehicle owners.
🔵
Zero Depreciation Add-on
Full claim without depreciation deduction. Recommended for new vehicles under 5 years old.
Renewal Process
01
Visit Insurance Company / Agent
Or renew online via insurer's website. Carry RC, existing policy, and vehicle details.
02
Vehicle Inspection (if lapsed)
If policy lapsed over 90 days, physical inspection may be required before renewal.
03
Pay Premium & Receive Policy
Digital policy issued instantly. Save on DigiLocker or mParivahan app.

RC / Vehicle Registration Book

RC Renewal
🚗
Re-registration After 15 Years
Non-transport vehicles: valid for 15 years. After 15 years, mandatory re-registration + fitness test required.
🔄
Transport Vehicles: 5 Years
Transport/commercial vehicle RC valid for 5 years. Fitness certificate needed for each renewal.
Documents for RC Update
Original RC Book
With all entries up to date
Valid Insurance Certificate
Mandatory for RC renewal process
PUC Certificate
Valid pollution certificate required
Fitness Certificate (HMV)
Mandatory for transport and heavy vehicles

Re-test / Driving Test Again

When is Re-test Required?
❌
Failed Driving Test
If you fail the RTO practical test, you can apply for re-test after 7 days. Test fee ₹300.
⏳
DL Expired Over 1 Year
If license expired more than 1 year ago, fresh practical test required before renewal is granted.
🚫
License Suspended/Revoked
Court or RTO may order driving test before reinstating a suspended/revoked license.
Re-test Process
01
Book Appointment Online
Visit parivahan.gov.in → Services → DL → Test Slot Booking.
02
Practice with Bismi MDS
Enroll in our Refresher Course to regain confidence and pass the test first time.
03
Appear at RTO Test Ground
Bring original documents, wear seatbelt, demonstrate safe driving skills at SRTO Ponkunnam.
Pro Tip: Bismi MDS offers targeted re-test preparation coaching. Call +91 85929 81182 for details.

PUC — Pollution Under Control

🌿
Validity Period
New vehicles: 1 year validity. Older vehicles: 6 months. Available at petrol pumps and authorized centers.
💰
Cost
₹80–₹100 for two-wheelers. ₹100–₹150 for cars. ₹150–₹200 for diesel vehicles.
⚖️
Fine for No PUC
₹1,000 for first offense. ₹2,000 for repeat. Vehicle may be impounded if PUC fails the emission test.
🌱
Renew Your PUC

Visit any authorized fuel station or PUC center. Only takes 5 minutes. Keep the digital certificate on mParivahan app.
